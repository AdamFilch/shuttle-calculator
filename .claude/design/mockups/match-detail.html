Match detail, redesigned

The page you land on after tapping a match on Session detail. It reuses the Create match court, read-only, so everyone sees exactly where each player stood. A result card above it records who won and an optional score, and the court shows the winning side. Below it are the shuttles used in this match, grouped by type and by whether each one was new, reused or free. Sample: Thursday doubles, tonight's open session.
