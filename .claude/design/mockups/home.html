Home, redesigned

Home is the first screen the manager sees when they open the app, at the court or at home afterwards. Today it leads with one big "Total outstanding" figure, which repeats what player and session detail already do. This board starts with a brainstorm of what Home could hold, then three ways to lay it out. No section on Home uses a single huge number. Layout directions

Three ways to put the brainstorm together. Each is a full Home screen on the same sample data. Switch between them below.

A · Action-first (approved)

Home answers "what do I do next" from top to bottom: the session, then anything that needs attention, then context. Sections that have nothing to say hide themselves, so on a quiet day Home is short.

Data for direction A

  • Exists: session card (fetchAllSessions + previewSessionCharges), owers and oldest debt (fetchAllPlayerPayments), recent sessions (fetchAllSessions), stock left (fetchAllShuttlesWithInventory), setup checklist (existing list fetches).
  • New query: shuttle runway. Average shuttles used per session for each type over the last 5 closed sessions, so the UI can say "about 1 session". Low = 2 sessions or fewer, falling back to today's 2-shuttle rule when there's no history.
  • New query: 30-day summary. Sessions, matches, distinct players, shuttles used, total charged and still due between two dates.
  • Nice to have: top owers. A small query returning owed amount and oldest unpaid date per player, sorted, instead of deriving it from the full payments list in the UI.
  • Removed: InsightsSection (chart) leaves Home. Moving it to the Shuttles tab is follow-up work outside this design.

B · Dashboard (not chosen)

Home as a club overview: numbers first, then a compact chart, money, stock and sessions. Closest to what you first pictured, and the strongest for a weekly review. It is the weakest at the court, because starting or continuing a session shrinks to a header button.

Data for direction B

  • Exists: owers, recent sessions, stock left, the open session.
  • New query: 30-day summary (same as A).
  • New query: shuttles per session for the last 8 sessions (today's series is by day, week or month, not by session).
  • New query: shuttle runway (same as A), for every type rather than only low ones.

C · Feed (not chosen)

Home as a timeline of what happened, newest first: sessions closed, payments taken, stock bought, stock running low. Good at "what changed since I last looked". Weak at "who owes right now", because debt is scattered across days.

Data for direction C

  • Exists: the session card, stock left.
  • New query: activity feed. A union over sessions (closed_date), payments (date_paid), shuttle_purchases.date and matches per session, newest first and paged. All dates already exist; grouping paid charges into one "paid RM X" line per player per day needs care.
  • New query: shuttle runway (same as A).
